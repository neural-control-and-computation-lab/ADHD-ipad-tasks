NCC Lab iPad tasks

Tasks for markerless motion-tracking experiments (ATHENA). Each one runs in Safari on an iPad, flashes white patches in the screen corners so it can be lined up with the camera video, and saves a CSV log on the iPad. Open a task, then use Share → Add to Home Screen to run it full screen.

Go/no-go reach taskReach and tap the circle for X, don't move for A. Practice block plus 4 test blocks, with random fixation times.
Storytelling taskTwo silent films and two spoken stories in random order, each followed by a timed retell with a countdown.
