# Retell times

How long participants have to retell each film and story in the storytelling task. Change them here: no coding needed.

### How to change a time

1. On GitHub, click the pencil icon ("Edit this file") at the top right of this file.
2. Change the time after `Retell time:`. Keep the headings (the lines starting with `##`) and the words `Retell time:`.
3. Click **Commit changes…**, then **Commit changes** again in the box that opens.
4. After about 2 minutes, check the [preview page](https://neural-control-and-computation-lab.github.io/ADHD-ipad-tasks/preview.html), which lists the times. Each iPad uses the new times the next time the storytelling task is opened with internet.

### Good to know

- A time can be written as `80`, `80 seconds`, `1 minute`, `1.5 minutes` or `1:20`.
- The countdown on the iPad and `{retell time}` in [participant-text.md](participant-text.md) follow these times. If the times differ, `{retell time}` on the introduction screen says "between 60 and 90 seconds" (for example).
- The headings must start with Film 1, Film 2, Story 1 or Story 2. The title after the colon is only a reminder.
- Every data file records the retell time used for each film and story.
- If something here can't be read, the app uses its built-in time for that film or story (80 seconds) and the preview page says what to fix.
