# Retell times

How long participants have to retell each film and story. You can change them here without any coding.

### How to make a change

1. Click the pencil icon at the top right of this file.
2. Change the time after `Retell time:` for the film or story you want.
3. Click the green **Commit changes…** button, then **Commit changes** in the box that opens. This saves it.
4. Wait about 2 minutes, then open the [preview page](https://neural-control-and-computation-lab.github.io/ADHD-ipad-tasks/preview.html). It lists the four times. If it still shows the old ones, wait a minute and press Reload.

The iPads use the new times the next time the storytelling task is opened (with internet).

### What to change

Each film and story below looks like this:

```
  ## Film 1: The Book Witch      ← heading: keep "Film 1" (the title is only a reminder)
  Retell time: 80 seconds        ← change only the time
```

- You can write the time as `90`, `90 seconds`, `1.5 minutes` or `1:30`.
- The countdown and `{retell time}` in [participant-text.md](participant-text.md) follow these times by themselves. If the times differ, the first screen says "between 60 and 90 seconds" (for example).
- If the app can't read a time, it uses 80 seconds for that one, and the preview page says what to fix.
- Each data file records the time each film and story had.
- Every saved version of this file is kept. **History** (top right of the file) lists them. To undo a change, ask Jonathan, or open the earlier version from History and copy its text back in.
